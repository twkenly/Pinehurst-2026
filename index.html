Home Base

St. Andrews #139

Sleeping Four
Three bedrooms for four of us — everybody gets their own bed, with two of us sharing the double-queen room. Two full bathrooms. Deck with a delightful view of the 3rd hole of Pinehurst No. 5. Full kitchen, washer/dryer, and room to spare.
Photos are representative — the condos are individually owned, so our actual unit may differ.
The Rhythm
Breakfast at the Carolina Hotel or Pl8te every morning, shuttle to the tee, shower back at the condo, then out to dinner. Nothing is more than fifteen minutes away.
Logistics

Getting There

Wednesday Night
Tyler + Kyle · The Westin RDU Airport
Two-queen guest room. Tyler checks in that morning and adds Kyle as an authorized guest. Head straight there after you land; Tyler's back late from dinner in Chapel Hill.
On the Ground
Tyler and Kyle collect the rental car Thursday morning, grab Granger at RDU on the way, and it carries us all weekend. Inside the resort, call the shuttle for transpo within Pinehurst Resort. Tobacco Road to RDU is about 45 minutes.
